Crown AI — one-click amendment capture

The build environment cannot reach planning.vic.gov.au. Your browser can. This captures the page you are looking at, with its bytes and their fingerprint, into a file the pipeline accepts.

It runs entirely in your browser and makes no network requests. Nothing is sent anywhere — the only thing that leaves is a file you save yourself. The source is tools/capture-bookmarklet.js, and it is short enough to read.

1. Install it

Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+B), then drag this onto it:

Crown capture

Dragging is required — clicking it here does nothing useful, because there is no amendment page to read. If your browser will not accept the drag, open the console on the amendment page instead and paste the contents of tools/capture-bookmarklet.js.

2. Capture one page per LGA

These are the three that acceptance criterion 1 needs — one real, current amendment from each LGA:

LGAAmendmentPage
HumeC272humehttps://planning-schemes.app.planning.vic.gov.au/Hume/amendments/C272hume
MeltonC232melthttps://planning-schemes.app.planning.vic.gov.au/Melton/amendments/C232melt
WyndhamC266wyndhttps://planning-schemes.app.planning.vic.gov.au/Wyndham/amendments/C266wynd
Two of these are marked CONTRADICTED in the lead file: independent searches disagreed about them. C232melt had two different gazettal dates and C272hume two different descriptions. Trust the page in front of you, not the values the panel suggests.

On each page: click Crown capture, check every field in the panel against what you can see, tick the confirmation, and save. You get one file per page.

3. Send them back

Attach the saved crown-capture-*.json files to the conversation, or run:

python -m ingest.cli --lga Wyndham --capture crown-capture-*.json --as hawktrust1@gmail.com

One command takes all three. Each capture closes the queued lead for its amendment, and each becomes evidence graded OPERATOR_CAPTURE — strong enough that a gazetted amendment counts as a FACT, never AUTHORITATIVE, which stays reserved for a fetch the system makes itself.
